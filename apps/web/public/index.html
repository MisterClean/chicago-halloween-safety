What the interval means

We resample the eight years 20,000 times, keeping each Halloween paired with its comparison days. The 95% interval describes variation across years. With only eight Halloweens, it is approximate. Sparse fatality totals do not receive a bootstrap interval. Measures are exploratory and intervals are not corrected for multiple comparisons.

04 · Evidence and limits

Methods, coverage and reproducibility

What we count

Crashes are distinct crash records. Total injuries include fatal, incapacitating, non-incapacitating, and reported but not evident injuries. “Serious” here means the police classification of incapacitating injury. Fatalities are reported deaths, not fatal crashes. Pedestrian and child outcomes use the People table; children have recorded ages 0–17. Missing ages are not assigned to an age group.

People are aggregated to crash ID before joining. Overall injuries use crash totals; pedestrian and child injuries use person classifications. Any disagreements are reported in the audit. Timestamps are Chicago local civil time.

Which records qualify

Crashes have citywide CPD coverage from September 2017. The principal comparisons use complete 2018–2025 years. Earlier partial coverage and the ongoing 2026 year are excluded. Interstate, ramp, and boundary-road crashes handled by other agencies may be absent. Reports can be amended, so this is a dated snapshot.

A car-free permit must be a BlockParty application, have an issue date and a Complete milestone, and specify Full street closure. Denied and cancelled applications are excluded. We exclude malformed dates and durations of three days or more. Permits issued after the event’s first calendar day are excluded. Distinct applications are counted once per ward and calendar day, even when they have several location records.

Statistical details and robustness

The calendar chart excludes February 29 so every plotted date has eight years of exposure. Rankings use competition ranks and report ties. The weekday comparison is a descriptive count ratio, not an exposure-adjusted risk ratio. Calendar-placebo tests reassign the event label among the Halloween date and its matched dates in each year, using 50,000 draws; those dates are not randomized treatment assignments. Placebo values are exploratory.

The ward maps change May 18, 2015 and May 15, 2023. We retrieve 2003, 2015 and 2023 polygons, discard the 2003 “OUT” polygons, and assign longitude/latitude points to numbered wards. A point exactly on a shared boundary is assigned the lowest matching ward number. Permit points describe the start of an address range, which can cross a ward line.
